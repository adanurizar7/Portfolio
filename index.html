Commercial awareness

Manufacturing & logistics under margin pressure

Manufacturers are under constant pressure to squeeze cost-per-man-hour and throughput without added headcount — the exact problem I worked on daily at Shaw. Consulting and analytics teams that serve industrial clients need people who've seen a shift-level P&L conversation happen in real time, not just in a case study.

Analytics is moving from reporting to prescribing

Dashboards that just report what happened are table stakes now; the value is in tools and analyses that tell a supervisor or a client what to do next. That's the shift I made building the Shaw KPI system, and it's the same shift consulting/analyst work asks for at a larger scale.
